Install ClearTrack as a standalone app

Once installed, ClearTrack will appear on your desktop or home screen and can be launched like a regular app, without opening a browser.

Desktop (Chrome/Edge):

Look for the install icon ➕ in your browser's address bar and click it, then select "Install".

Android (Chrome):

Tap the menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" or look for the install banner at the bottom of the screen.

iPhone/iPad (Safari):

Tap the Share button □↑ → "Add to Home Screen" → "Add".
