Get started with ClearTrack See what ClearTrack can do

ClearTrack is a private app built and operated in South Africa. It does not ask for or use any SARS eFiling, banking, or employer login credentials.

Install ClearTrack like an app on your phone or computer

Once installed, ClearTrack will appear on your desktop or home screen and can be launched like a regular app, without opening a browser.

Desktop (Chrome/Edge):

Look for the install icon ➕ in your browser's address bar and click it, then select "Install".

Android (Chrome):

Tap the menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" or look for the install banner at the bottom of the screen.

iPhone/iPad (Safari):

Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen" → "Add".

Key features

Mileage logbook

Capture trips with dates, odometer readings, business purpose and route details that match SARS guidelines.

Vehicle & expense tracking

Link trips to specific vehicles and keep records of fuel, services and repairs for audit support.

Document storage

Upload supporting documents (logbooks, IRP5s, invoices, etc.) so everything is in one place at tax time.

Practitioner-friendly export

Share SARS-style summaries and exports with your tax practitioner or accountant securely.

Who ClearTrack is for

Whether you're claiming travel or helping clients, ClearTrack fits your world.

Employees with travel allowances

Keep a compliant logbook for code 3701/3702 travel allowances and avoid surprises when SARS assesses your return.

Commission earners & sales reps

Track business vs private kilometres accurately and support claims against commission income.

Small business owners

Maintain proper records for vehicle expenses and travel costs to support your business tax return.

Tax practitioners

Access client logbooks and documents in one place, with SARS-ready exports to streamline your workflow.

How ClearTrack works

  1. Create an account and add your vehicle(s).
  2. Capture trips or import your route list / call cycle.
  3. Upload supporting documents such as fuel slips and IRP5s.
  4. Share exports with your practitioner when it is time to file.
